Nearest neighbor is about 2 to 3 times faster since it only reads one pixel, but edges come out jagged and blocky, especially where the image gets stretched like the sign. Bilinear averages the 4 nearest pixels so it is slower but much smoother.

A.4: Blend the Images into a Mosaic

Subway

Deli

Street

I keep the middle image fixed (the second one when there are only two) and warp the others into its frame with bilinear warping, on a canvas big enough to fit all the warped corners. Each image gets a weight that is 1 at its center and falls off linearly to 0 at its edges, warped along with the image. The mosaic is the weighted average of the images, so the seams fade out instead of one image overwriting another.
